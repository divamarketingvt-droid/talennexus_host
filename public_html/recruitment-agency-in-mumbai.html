07 Buyer's Guide

How to choose a recruitment agency in Mumbai

Compare every recruitment agency, employment agency or HR agency in Mumbai on the same six points before you sign. Tick them off as you speak to each one.

  • Industry experience: ask for hires the agency has made in your sector and role level.
  • Turnaround: ask for timelines per hiring model, not one headline number.
  • Replacement guarantee: check the period and what counts as an early exit.
  • Compliance: for contract staff, confirm who handles PF, ESI, Maharashtra PT and LWF.
  • Screening: ask how candidates are assessed — technical tests, psychometrics, background checks.
  • Single point of contact: one account manager who knows your business saves time on every hire.
