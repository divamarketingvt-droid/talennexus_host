What We Measure

Choose the areas that matter for the role — from entry-level screening to leadership hiring.

Aptitude

Numerical, verbal and logical reasoning — how quickly and accurately someone learns and solves problems.

Personality & Work Style

How a person prefers to work, make decisions and deal with people.

Behavioural Competencies

Teamwork, ownership, customer focus and other behaviours the role needs.

Emotional Intelligence

Self-awareness, managing pressure and working well with others.

Situational Judgement

How candidates would handle realistic situations from the job.

Role & Culture Fit

How well a candidate’s profile matches the role and your way of working.
