How publishing works

  1. Write your post and fill the SEO fields on the right.
  2. Set Status → Published and click Save.
  3. The post is live immediately at /blog/your-url, on the blog page and in the home page blog section.
  4. Keep a post as Draft to save it without showing it to visitors.

Tip: submit talennexus.com/blog/sitemap.xml once in Google Search Console.
