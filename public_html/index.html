Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about partnering with Talennexus for your HR and talent management needs.

General

Talennexus operates as a strategic HR partner—not just a service provider. We offer a 360-degree suite covering recruitment, staffing, interviews & assessments, payroll, campus hiring and Virtual HR under one roof. Our approach is goal-aligned: we bridge the gap between your organizational objectives and workforce potential, rather than simply filling open positions.

We serve a diverse range of sectors including Healthcare (Hospitals, Pharma, MedTech), Telecom (ISP & 5G Networks), Retail (E-Commerce, Fashion, FMCG, D2C), Banking (Commercial Banks, NBFCs, Fintech, Insurance), FMCG (Manufacturing & Supply Chain), and Technology (IT Services, SaaS, AI/ML). Each industry receives specialized attention tailored to its unique workforce demands.

Our agile staffing model is designed for speed without compromising quality. For contract and temporary staffing, we can deploy pre-vetted professionals within 48–72 hours for common skill sets. For specialized or leadership roles, our executive search process typically delivers shortlisted candidates within 2–3 weeks, depending on role complexity.
Services

We manage end-to-end payroll processing including salary calculations, deductions, bonuses, and expense reimbursements. Every operation adheres strictly to local labor laws, tax regulations, and statutory compliance requirements. We also integrate employee benefits, health insurance, and PTO tracking into the payroll cycle, significantly reducing your legal and compliance risks.
