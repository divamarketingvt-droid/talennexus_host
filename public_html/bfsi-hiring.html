Our domain assessment is a dual-layer process. Layer 1 is a standardized BFSI domain test covering regulatory knowledge, industry concepts, and role-specific technical questions (e.g., credit appraisal methodology, insurance underwriting principles, SEBI regulations). Layer 2 is a panel interview conducted by our in-house BFSI SMEs — former bankers, insurance leaders, or capital market professionals with 15+ years experience. For senior roles, we also use case study-based evaluations simulating real-world scenarios like loan portfolio stress testing, claims fraud detection, or regulatory response planning.
