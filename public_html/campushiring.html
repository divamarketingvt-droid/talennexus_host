How Campus Nexus Works

A structured 4-step journey from enrollment to placement

1
Assessment & Customization

Students undergo an initial skill assessment. Based on results, a customized training roadmap is created aligned with the target industry and company requirements.

2
Intensive Training Phase

9-module training covering technical skills, AI tools, communication, aptitude, and professional grooming with continuous mock assessments and feedback loops.

3
6-Week Live Project

Students work on real-world projects in teams, applying all learned skills. Mentors from the industry guide them through sprint planning, execution, and presentation.

4
Campus Drives & Placement

Trained and project-ready students participate in organized campus drives with our 50+ hiring partners, completing the journey from campus to career.
