07 Buyer's Guide

How to choose the best IT consultancy in Pune for hiring

Compare IT recruitment agencies in Pune on the same six points before you sign. Tick them off as you speak to each one.

  • Tech depth: ask which stacks and seniority levels the agency has actually hired for.
  • Technical screening: confirm who tests candidates and how — interview, coding test or both.
  • Turnaround: ask for timelines per hiring model, and how they handle 60–90 day notice periods.
  • Replacement guarantee: check the period and what counts as an early exit.
  • Contract compliance: for contract engineers, confirm who handles PF, ESI, PT and LWF.
  • Single point of contact: one account manager who knows your team saves time on every hire.
