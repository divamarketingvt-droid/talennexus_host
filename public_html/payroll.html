Our Payroll Process

A systematic, error-proof workflow that ensures every payday is flawless.

01
Setup

Data Onboarding & Configuration

We collect your employee master data, salary structures, statutory registrations, and configure your payroll rules, pay components, and approval workflows.

02
Input

Monthly Data Collection

Attendance, leave, overtime, new joinees, exits, arrears, and variable pay inputs are gathered through automated feeds or a simple upload interface.

03
Process

Payroll Computation & Validation

Salary is computed with all statutory deductions, variable pay, and reimbursements. Multi-level validation checks catch discrepancies before payout.

04
Disburse

Approved payroll is disbursed via direct bank transfer. Salary slips, tax reports, and compliance filings are generated and distributed automatically.

05
Support

Post-Payroll Support & Reconciliation

We handle employee queries, bank reconciliation, corrections if any, and prepare audit-ready documentation for the cycle.
